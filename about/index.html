ABOUT

Hi, my name is Amy and I am a graphic and UI/UX designer.
I work in the following tools:

  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Adobe InDesign
  • Adobe After Effects
  • Figma

My creative journey began early in my life. I was one of the most artistically skilled people in my class, however, my interest over time has shifted from fine arts to digital design.

I started off making digital video and photo edits, and soon afterwards I branched off into graphic design and UI/UX projects. I am a perfectionist and love to keep things organized, so I try to keep my designs as clean and orderly as possible while making them purposeful.

Thanks for stopping by :)
