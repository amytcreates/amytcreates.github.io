Goal & Purpose
The goal of this project was to redesign and illustrate the container of a Pepsi brand project. I chose to redesign O.N.E., a coconut water brand. I wanted the design to utilise the brand's original aesthetic, while reinventing the bottle to something more unique that reflects the essence of the product.
