Goal & Purpose
The goal was to design a magazine using a systematic approach and to apply the system to each corresponding spread or page. The magazine has to be visually appealing and purposeful. The magazine sections have to have their own distinct look and feel while also borrowing some elements from the front cover. For this project, I designed a lifestyle magazine called Resplendent. The content of this magazine aims to make people smile and feel happy, while also exploring deeper topics relating to mental health and well-being. The magazine style is vibrant and colorful, but also informative.
Subject Research

Magazine design has to be intentional but also engaging to the reader. It has to be visually appealing and immerse the reader. It all starts with a cover, because when you have a well-designed cover, the reader will be more willing to pick the magazine up. Then it involves great use of text and image. The ability to combine text and image is very important because this is one way to make the magazine visually appealing and also helps to engage the reader, providing them with clues as to what the magazine is about. It is also important that the images do not distract or take too much away from the text.


A magazine typically has a cover that includes a masthead, table of contents, articles including the main feature, ads, and departments. A masthead includes essential information about the magazine. It is also the main branding for the magazine, whether it comes in the form of a logo, an image, text or a combination of all of those. Departments are distinct from the rest of the magazine in their system and style, however their content somehow are related in some way. They could contain a letter from the editorial staff, or extra stories that are not as important as the content in the feature article.

Cover 1
Resplendent currently has three issues, each one exhibiting a different front cover. The first one is issued during the summer, and features a beautiful sunset view with a cascading sky of colors, and palm trees. The masthead rests horizontally on the left side of the cover, allowing the cover image to be highlighted and emphasized. The back cover is an ad for Banana Boat sunscreen.
Cover 2
The second cover is issued during autumn, and features trees starting to change orange and yellow. There is some sunlight seeping through the tree branches, signaling that summer is not fully over yet, but is also starting to come to an end. Usually, Replendent would have content relating to each season or issue, but I only have the summer issue fully designed which is why the content is the same except for the front covers.
Cover 3
The third cover is issued during the winter, and features a nice view of a sunrise or sunset along with the ground and tree branches covered in snow.
Table of Contents
The table of contents spread contains the magazine sections listed on one page and the feature spread highlighted slightly larger on the other page.
Department & Half Page Ad
The department contains a list of inspirational and uplifting quotes. There is also a half-page ad for a gratitude journal.
Intro to Feature
This page is a leadup to the main feature article. It gives a sneak peak into what the feature is going to be about.
Feature
The feature is an insightful and informative list of self care tips to keep in mind during the summer. Each tip is highlighted in all caps and red color. There are also some related images accompanying each page.
Follow-Up Spread
This page is a follow-up to the feature and contains a fun selection of flowers that could be used in a garden. Each flower has its own image and description. The ad on the right page is for an app called PictureThis. This app can identify a plant in seconds using a camera or image.
