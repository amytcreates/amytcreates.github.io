Goal & Purpose
The goal of this project was to create a cohesive campaign for a non-profit organization. I chose the National Harm Reduction Coalition and created Carry Naloxone, a campaign which aims to encourage people of all ages to carry naloxone, otherwise known as Narcan, with them when they travel or go out, and raise awareness of what naloxone can do when they encounter somebody who is experiencing an overdose or are experiencing an overdose themselves. The campaign consists of a full-scale poster, three magazine ads, one public transportation application, a website landing page, and an additional item of choice that includes a unique way of sharing the campaign's message.
Poster
The campaign poster features bold typography and impactful imagery to capture viewers and convey the serious nature of the opioid crisis. The design uses a stark color contrast to emphasize the urgency of the message. The headline highlights the 'Nalox' and 'Nar' in red, leaving 'One' and 'Can' in white. A play on words, the white type subsequently reads, 'Carrying One Can Save Lives,' reinforcing the campaign's message about stopping drug overdose. The poster features a hand spraying a Naloxone nasal spray, as well as QR codes that link to informational resources.
Campaign Magazine Ads
There are three variations for magazine ads. The first is the main poster design. The other two utilize the same design as the first, replacing the featured image with a different image or form of Naloxone. The magazine advertisements expand on the campaign's message with detailed information about prevention, treatment options, and support resources. Each ad is designed to be informative while maintaining visual impact.
Additional Item
The additional item created for Carry Naloxone is a large-scale transportation sign. The concept of this sign is that men and women would typically carry different items with them as they travel or go out, however, Naloxone is a universal item that should be carried by everyone if possible. QR codes are displayed on the bottom for informational resources.
Website: Landing Page
A redesigned landing page for the National Harm Reduction Coalition. The campaign landing page serves as a central hub for information, providing visitors with educational resources, statistics, and ways to get involved or seek help. It has a bold headline and accompanying sub-headline. Below those are links to learn more and find Naloxone in a nearby area, and a link to donate to the organization.
Website: Donate
A dedicated donation page features a grey background banner of people holding up protest signs. It encourages visitors to support and donate to the organization, which helps to transform countless lives by fighting the opioid crisis and supporting recovery programs.
Website: Find Naloxone
An interactive page helps visitors locate nearby sources for Naloxone, whether they want syringes or Naloxone mailed directly to them. This page provides immediate access to emergency resources.
