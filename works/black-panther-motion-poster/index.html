Goal & Purpose
The goal of this project was to create and animate a movie poster. I decided to do one for Black Panther, one of my favorite movies. The animation has to reflect the movie in a way that gives an idea as to what the movie is about without the use of words and has to reflect the movie's tone and atmosphere.
Description
I created a saber or neon outline fire effect that moves along the frame of the main figure. There are flames and sparks from a fire that appears in the background. The movie's logo zooms in from the bottom, then zooms out when the animation ends. I thought the fire effects I added reflects the movie's atmosphere and intensity. It gives the impression of an action movie, which it is considered to be.
